7 Preschool Milestones Worth Celebrating With a Certificate

Every child grows at their own pace, but a few milestones are worth marking with something a little more special than a sticker. Finishing preschool, learning the alphabet, counting to 100, or becoming potty-trained are all achievements that build a child's confidence when they're recognized.

A printed certificate gives children something tangible to hold onto — many parents display them on the fridge or in a keepsake box, and children often remember the moment they received one long after the milestone itself has passed.

Milestones to consider:

  • Completing preschool or a learning program
  • Learning the alphabet from A to Z
  • Counting to 20, 100, or beyond
  • Potty training success
  • Reading their first book independently
  • Consistent good behavior over a set period
  • Overcoming a fear, like the first day without a parent in the room
