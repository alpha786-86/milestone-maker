About KidCertificates

KidCertificates started with a simple idea: celebrating a child's small wins shouldn't require design skills or expensive software. Whether it's finishing preschool, learning the alphabet, or mastering good behavior, every milestone deserves a moment of recognition.

Our free certificate maker lets parents, teachers, and homeschoolers create a personalized, printable certificate in under a minute — no design experience needed.

We're a small, independently run project focused on making early-childhood milestones a little more special, one certificate at a time.
